B.D.B.K.S COLLEGE FORBESGANJ ARARIA was established in 1981 and it is managed by the Department of Education. It is located in Rural area. It is located in FORBISGANJ block of ARARIA district of Bihar. The school consists of Grades from 9 to 12. The school is Co-educational and it doesn't have an attached pre-primary section. The school is N/A in nature and is not using school building as a shift-school. Hindi is the medium of instructions in this school. This school is approachable by all weather road. In this school academic session starts in April.
